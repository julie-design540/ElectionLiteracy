Participation checklist

Before election day

  • Confirm your registration details and polling station early.
  • Compare manifestos and candidate records against local needs.
  • Discuss issues respectfully and avoid sharing unverified claims.
During voting

At the polling station

  • Carry the correct ID and follow queue and verification instructions.
  • Mark your ballot privately and ask officials for procedural guidance.
  • Keep the process peaceful and report irregularities through official channels.
After elections

Stay accountable

  • Track public promises, budgets, and service delivery timelines.
  • Attend participation forums and submit evidence-based feedback.
  • Use civic art and lessons to keep public education active year round.
