Before you upload

Make the civic message clear

  • Use a strong title that reflects the election or civic theme.
  • Choose a category that matches the message of the artwork.
  • Upload a clean image or add a verified video link with a clear civic message.
  • After submission you will land on a confirmation page with a direct link to the item.

Voting responsibly

Art that educates should also encourage voters to:

  • Verify candidate information before casting your vote.
  • Respect polling station rules and bring your ID.
  • Share civic messages that build trust and participation.
