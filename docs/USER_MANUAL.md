# User Manual

## What This Platform Is

This website is a civic education and election awareness platform. It helps Kenyans learn through visual content instead of long text-only materials.

## Register

Open the registration page, enter a username, email, and password, then submit the form.

## Login

Use your username or email with your password to sign in.

## Browse Artwork

Use the gallery to search, filter by category, and sort civic artwork. The content may include posters, infographics, campaign graphics, and election explainers.

## Upload Artwork

After logging in, open the upload page, add a title, description, category, and image, then submit.

## Interact

On the artwork detail page you can like, share, favourite, comment on, download, or report artwork. These actions help the community surface useful civic material, and you can also see who engaged with each post.

## Profile

The profile page lets you edit your details and change your password.

## Upload Artwork

After logging in, open the upload page to submit your own civic artwork. After submission, the artwork will be reviewed by an administrator before it appears in the public gallery.
